## Cross-curriculum design findings

1. The books are not uniform. The knowledge system needs per-source metadata and page-aware retrieval.
2. Several major sources are image-based in the current parser. A text-only RAG design would fail or lose important content.
3. Images, maps, diagrams, formulas, tables, and page layout are educational content, especially in Math, Science, Social Studies, Arabic, and English.
4. The curriculum itself already emphasizes skills, application, communication, reasoning, and higher-order thinking in multiple subjects. LUMINA's understanding-first philosophy is therefore compatible with the supplied books.
5. Subject mastery should not use one universal concept schema. Each subject needs common fields plus subject-specific skill dimensions.
6. School English and Real English should remain separate but share a learner language profile.
7. School ICT and AI/Python enrichment should remain separate but can cross-link deliberately.
8. Source provenance must be stored at least as: subject, academic metadata shown in file, term/semester, unit/chapter, lesson/topic, source file, and page/range.
9. Future uploads need classification as temporary material vs trusted permanent learning source.
